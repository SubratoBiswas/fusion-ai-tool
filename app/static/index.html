AI Protocol Designer

Describe a study concept and generate a structured protocol synopsis — objectives, design, eligibility criteria, endpoints, statistics, and safety monitoring. Drafts require medical-writer and regulatory review.

AI Eligibility Screener

Paste a patient profile and the protocol's criteria. The AI assesses each criterion and flags missing information. Decision support only — investigators confirm final eligibility.

AI Medical Coding

Map verbatim adverse event terms (as written by sites) to MedDRA-style Preferred Terms and System Organ Classes, with a confidence flag for coder review.

AI Data Quality Review

Run automated edit checks across a study's EDC data — missing values, eligibility deviations, AE coding gaps, seriousness/severity mismatches — then get an AI-written data review narrative. Findings become open queries.

Registry Intelligence

Search ClinicalTrials.gov for competitive landscape during feasibility — with an AI summary of phases, status mix, and enrollment competition.
